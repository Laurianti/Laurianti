# Federico Laurianti

CTO and Cloud Solution Architect at [Extra-Net](https://www.extranetsrl.it), in Turin. I work with .NET, Azure, Kubernetes and AI, and I teach cloud and AI at ITS Academies.

- I maintain [ChromaDotNet](https://chromadotnet.org), the [.NET client](https://github.com/ChromaDotNet/ChromaDB.Client) for [Chroma](https://www.trychroma.com/) and [Chroma Cloud](https://docs.trychroma.com/cloud/getting-started), and [ChromaDotNet.Local](https://github.com/ChromaDotNet/ChromaDB.Local), Chroma on your machine without Docker, and [ChromaDotNet.Embeddings](https://github.com/ChromaDotNet/ChromaDB.Embeddings), the default embedding function of Chroma for .NET.
- Member of the [.NET Foundation](https://dotnetfoundation.org).
